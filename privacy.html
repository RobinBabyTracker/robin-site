Robin privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

The short version. Robin helps families with a new baby ask each other for a hand, plan events, chat and keep memories. We collect only what Robin needs to work. Your family's information is visible only to the people you invite. We don't sell your data, show ads or use tracking or analytics tools, and you can delete your account at any time.

1. Who we are

Robin ("Robin", "we", "us") is an app made by Amber Ferris in Queensland, Australia. If you have any questions about this policy or your information, email robin.babytracker@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only in a securely scrambled (hashed) form that we can't read.
  • Profile: your time zone, and optionally a name your family uses for you, such as "Nana".
  • Family details: the families you create or join and your role in each (owner, parent or helper). If you invite someone, we store a scrambled copy of the invite code, the role you chose, when it expires and an optional private note.
  • What you share:
    • Help requests and events: titles, details, times, places, notes and links.
    • Replies to them.
    • Messages.
    • Photos and videos you add to chats or Memories, with their captions and dates.
  • Settings: your notification choices and quiet hours.

Information collected automatically

  • Device token for notifications: if you allow notifications, a token that lets us deliver them to your phone, and whether your phone is Android or iPhone.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider keeps short-term logs of requests, such as times, IP addresses and error details, to keep the service secure and working.
  • Security records: if someone enters wrong invite codes, we record the time against their account for up to one day, to stop codes being guessed.
  • On your phone: Robin stores your sign-in session and a few preferences on your device, such as whether you've seen the welcome tour.

What we don't collect

Robin doesn't ask for your location, contacts, advertising ID or payment details. It doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising tools. Children don't have accounts.

Photos and videos can contain hidden details from your phone, such as where they were taken. Robin doesn't read or use these details. Depending on your phone's settings, they may still be stored inside the file you share. You can turn off location tagging in your camera settings.

3. How we use your information

We use your information only to provide Robin:

  • to create and secure your account, and sign you in;
  • to show requests, events, chats and memories to the right people in your family;
  • to send you the notifications and emails you need, such as sign-in codes, password resets, and updates about requests you're involved in;
  • to keep Robin safe, prevent misuse and fix problems.

We don't sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or use your content to train AI models.

4. Who can see your information

  • Your family: people in a family can see its members, requests and events they're invited to, and shared memories. Memories can be marked "parents only".
  • Chats: only the people in a chat can see its messages and photos.
  • Declining a request: if you decline a request, only the person who asked can see that.
  • Photos and videos: these are stored privately. They open only through short-lived links for people allowed to see them.
  • Nobody outside your families can see anything you share in Robin.

6. How we protect it

  • All information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Database rules make sure people can only read what they're allowed to see. Photos and videos are kept in private storage.
  • Invite codes are single-use, expire, and are protected against guessing.

No online service can be perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you and, where required, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

7. How long we keep it

  • We keep your information while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account:
    • We remove your profile, email address, sign-in details, the photos and videos you uploaded, the memories you added, your notification settings and device tokens.
    • Your membership of every family ends, and requests you organised that haven't happened yet are cancelled.
  • Messages you sent stay in chats, shown as "Former member", so conversations still make sense to others.
  • Photos and videos you delete can no longer be opened by anyone, straight away.
  • Technical logs are kept by our hosting provider for a short period and then deleted automatically.

8. Children

Robin is for adults (18+). Children don't use Robin or have accounts. Parents decide which photos of their children to share, and only with people in their own family. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • see and update your name and time zone in You & family → Your profile;
  • delete messages and memories you've shared;
  • leave a family, or ask an owner to remove you;
  • change or turn off notifications in You & family → Notifications;
  • delete your account in You & family → Privacy & account, or by following our account deletion steps.

You can also ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing robin.babytracker@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, for example in the EU or UK, you may have extra rights, such as objecting to processing or asking for your data in a portable format. We'll honour these where they apply.

10. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please email us first and we'll try to fix it. If you're still not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au, or your local data protection authority.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll also let you know in the app or by email before they take effect.
